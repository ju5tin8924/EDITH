# Brag Plan: EDITH — Enterprise AML Intelligence Platform

## What is this app?
EDITH is a next-generation enterprise Anti-Money Laundering (AML) platform built for Tier-1 GCC financial institutions that screens high-velocity transactions in under 20 milliseconds across a 5-pass deterministic pipeline, guaranteeing zero Redis dependency and automated statutory freeze enforcement.

## The angle
High-velocity financial crime interception. In a world of multi-million dollar correspondent wires moving in milliseconds, compliance teams cannot wait for sluggish batch jobs or flaky Redis caches. EDITH intercepts designated sanctions targets (like Viktor Bout) in 8.4 milliseconds flat with token-level phonetic diagnostics.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-value cross-border wire transfer ($450,000 USD) hits the gateway. A high-precision latency counter counts up from 0.0ms and freezes at **8.4ms** as a glowing crimson **STATUTORY FREEZE** banner locks down the transaction.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1 (3-7s):** 5-Pass Deterministic Pipeline animation: Unicode Normalization → In-Memory Retrieval → Double Metaphone & Jaro-Winkler Phonetics → Multi-Role Screening → Whitelist Suppression.
- **Beat 2 (7-12s):** Diagnostic Token Diff: Live phonetic alignment showing incoming `"VICTOR"` matching UN/OFAC candidate `"VIKTOR"` at 94% confidence, and `"BOUT"` matching at 100% exact.
- **Beat 3 (12-16s):** Case Adjudication Canvas: Atomic pessimistic lock (`🔒 Locked by Fatima Al-Nuaimi`), 1-click UAE FIU goAML XML export, and FATF AI narrative copilot.

## Outro / punchline (16-20s)
The EDITH shield crest locks into place.
"78/78 Tests Passing. Zero Warnings. Monolithic PostgreSQL 17 Safety Net. Built for GCC Banking."
**EDITH v3.0 — The Speed of Compliance.**
